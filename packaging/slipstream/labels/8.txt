THE SHORTEST WAY                                   three steps, about 50 minutes, most of it hands off
  1. Double-click  Install-Slipstream.cmd          10 to 20 minutes, mostly one download of about 750 MB.
                                                   It asks ONE question (the optional NVIDIA neural
                                                   runtime: yes or no; "no" is fine, see section 1).
  2. Start the game on the PS5 and stand in a quiet place. Open a Command Prompt in this folder:
       Run-First-Test.cmd -Session input           about 21 minutes. Mouse, keyboard and the DualSense
                                                   (connected to this PC): the kit prints every step.
  3.   Run-First-Test.cmd -Session visual          about 25 minutes, hands off. Stand in ONE place with
                                                   detail in the picture and do not move. Your HDR
                                                   reference needs the H.265 HDR codec, chosen in the
                                                   PS5 connect dialog BEFORE this session (optional).
  Each session ends with RESULT.txt on screen and ONE zip to send back. That is all that is needed.
  Wired or Wi-Fi both run; on Wi-Fi every table says "Wi-Fi: diagnostic only".

WHAT IS NEW IN PACKAGE 8          (all of it in the experimental build, and OFF until you choose it)
  - Mouse and keyboard: a settings page with an on / off switch, bindings, calibration, and three input
    profiles (as today, Ultra Low Latency, Adaptive Precision). Session: input.
  - Restoration: a clean-up stage for the compressed 1080p stream in front of the upscaler to
    2560x1440, and five picture profiles. Session: visual.
  - Neural layer ("DLSS 5 NR" in the interface): optional and experimental, with a conservative look
    and a check that only an NVIDIA-signed original runtime is used. It is video processing, not
    NVIDIA's game-integrated DLSS 5. Sessions: visual, neural.
  - Frame generation, presentation and audio changes behind switches. Sessions: framegen, pacing,
    audiofix.
  - The kit: the time estimates were 4 times too high and are now taken from your runs of 20 November;
    RESULT.txt has a section MEASURED VALUES for every session.
----- the install question (section 1) -----
   THE ONE QUESTION: the neural layer needs NVIDIA's own runtime file. The installer keeps it only
   when you say yes, and only when the file is an NVIDIA-signed original; it never installs a
   modified file. Without it everything else works, and the neural arms of the kit say
   "not installed: skipped". To answer in advance:
       Install-Slipstream.cmd -NeuralRuntime Yes       (or No; Ask is the default)
