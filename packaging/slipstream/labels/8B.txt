THE SHORTEST WAY                                   three steps, about 50 minutes, most of it hands off
  1. Double-click  Install-Slipstream.cmd          10 to 20 minutes, mostly one download of about 750 MB.
                                                   No question. It updates an 8A in place (section 1).
  2. Start the game on the PS5 and stand in a quiet place. Open a Command Prompt in this folder:
       Run-First-Test.cmd -Session input           about 21 minutes. Mouse, keyboard and the DualSense
                                                   (connected to this PC): the kit prints every step.
  3.   Run-First-Test.cmd -Session pacing          about 8 minutes, hands off; then -Session audiofix
                                                   (about 19 minutes) when you have the time.
  Each session ends with RESULT.txt on screen and ONE zip to send back. That is all that is needed.
  Wired or Wi-Fi both run; on Wi-Fi every table says "Wi-Fi: diagnostic only".

WHAT IS IN PACKAGE 8B    (the second of three downloads; in the experimental build, OFF until chosen)
  - Everything of 8A: restoration, 1080p to 1440p, HDR handling, the picture profiles.
  - Mouse and keyboard: a settings page with an on / off switch, bindings, calibration, and three input
    profiles (as today, Ultra Low Latency, Adaptive Precision). Session: input.
  - Frame pacing, presentation, audio and reliability changes behind switches. Sessions: pacing,
    audiofix, framegen.
  - Motion at about 120 FPS that is only sometimes smooth: Session smoothness (about 17 minutes) shows
    how evenly frames are handed to the screen per arm (PresentMon, when installed: what was really
    displayed), not the FPS counter; your eyes decide. Nothing here is claimed to fix it.
ON WITHOUT A CHOICE IN 8B   (experimental build only; Package 7 and your own PushFPS unchanged)
  - Fixes taken from Veyra: the controller queue drops inputs that are too old, the neural layer's
    clean shutdown, and the loader fix for its DLL.
  - chiaki-ng fixes: reorder-queue drop, send-buffer teardown, HTTP header bounds, PIN log line.
  - The FPS readout says "N FPS shown" or "N FPS submitted" (and "uneven" when it is); "shown" is
    worked out from the present times, not read from the screen.
  - The variable-refresh state is read again 5 s and 15 s after the picture starts. If G-SYNC
    engages only then, variable-refresh presentation starts mid-session (before, at the next
    display change).
KNOWN ISSUE IN 8B
  - After using the profile "Ultra Low Latency" together with the lowest-latency switch, check that
    Smooth pacing is still how you want it (quick setup) before you measure anything else.
  - In the tables, the rows "in effect at the end" for the neural layer read n/a: it is not in 8B.
WHAT IS NOT IN 8B YET
  - 8C, the complete Package 8: the optional neural layer and its verified installation (session
    neural; the neural arms of -Session visual say "not in this build: skipped" in 8B).
