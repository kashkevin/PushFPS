THE SHORTEST WAY                                   four steps; the kit prints each session's time
  1. Double-click  Install-Slipstream.cmd          10 to 20 minutes. It updates your 8C or 8C1 copy in
                                                   place (settings and PS5 pairing stay) and asks ONE
                                                   question: the optional NVIDIA neural runtime (section 1).
  2. Start the game on the PS5 and play somewhere with steady motion. Open a Command Prompt here:
       Run-First-Test.cmd -Session smoothness      about 36 minutes, hands off but keep the camera moving
                                                   slowly. It compares 4K against 1440p upscaling, 6X
                                                   against a multiplier fitted to your screen, and
                                                   Smoothness Mode against a fixed setting, judged on
                                                   when frames were handed to the screen, not the counter.
  3.   Run-First-Test.cmd -Session visual -Arms c-15,c-30,c-50,c-100
                                                   The bitrate test: the PS5 is asked for 15, 30, 50 and
                                                   100 Mbit/s in turn (that run only; your saved console
                                                   profile is never changed). It shows what was asked,
                                                   what the console aimed for and what really arrived.
                                                   Keep the camera moving: a still picture needs few bits.
  4.   Run-First-Test.cmd -Session framegen -Arms nd-base
                                                   The network check, on the cable: are packets missing at
                                                   all? If missing, late, UDP errors, adapter discards and
                                                   lost frames all read 0, the network is not your problem.
  Each session ends with RESULT.txt on screen and ONE zip to send back. That is all that is needed.
  Optional: the picture comparison A to F on one recorded clip, and the camera test of real input
  delay: docs\HARDWARE_TEST_PROCEDURE.md and docs\CLARITY_VERDICT.md (sections 11 to 14).

  - Smoothness Mode (quick setup, off by default): aims at a steady 60, 90, 120 or 180 FPS from the
    GPU time it measures; it does not measure what the screen showed, and the engine can still skip

    hitches, not blur: the audit (docs\NETWORK_AUDIT.md) explains why.
  - New measurements ([slipstream-smooth], [slipstream-net]) and the bitrate test switch for the kit. Nothing in this
    package is claimed to make the picture sharper or the motion smoother before your sessions show it.

----- the install question (section 1) -----
   THE ONE QUESTION: the neural layer needs NVIDIA's own runtime file. The installer keeps it only
   when you say yes, and only when the file is an NVIDIA-signed original; it never installs a
   modified file. Without it everything else works, and the neural arms of the kit say
   "not installed: skipped". To answer in advance:
       Install-Slipstream.cmd -NeuralRuntime Yes       (or No; Ask is the default)
   UPDATING an earlier experimental copy (Package 7, 8A or 8B): an NVIDIA runtime copy that came with
   an earlier package without your yes is removed at this update, and a layer saved on it becomes
   unavailable. Answering no removes every copy, also one you added in Settings. Answer yes, or add
   it later in that copy's Settings, Components and licenses.
   THE OTHER THREE COPIES (original, stable, Package 7) use their older installers: on an NVIDIA PC
   they keep the NVIDIA-signed runtime file from Veyra's package without asking, as PushFPS 3.2.1
   does. Their neural layer still stays off until you turn it on.
