THE SHORTEST WAY                                   four steps, about 60 minutes, most of it hands off
  1. Double-click  Install-Slipstream.cmd          10 to 20 minutes, mostly one download of about 750 MB.
                                                   No question.
  2. Start the game on the PS5 and stand in a quiet place with detail in the picture (vegetation, HUD
     text, something dark). Open a Command Prompt in this folder:
       Run-First-Test.cmd -Session visual          about 25 minutes, hands off. Do not move. Your HDR
                                                   reference needs the H.265 HDR codec, chosen in the
                                                   PS5 connect dialog BEFORE this session (optional).
  3. LOOK WITH YOUR OWN EYES                       about 5 minutes, by hand, while you play.
     The numbers of -Session visual are software metrics; whether the picture is natural and sharp,
     without halos or ghosting, only your eyes on your screen can tell. Start the experimental copy
     (PushFPS-slipstream-exp\PushFPS.exe) yourself, connect, and in its quick setup choose the
     Picture profile "Original". Click the tile "Comparison clip": choose Layout "Side by side" (or
     "Split") and a Length, then "Start recording". Do it again with the profile "Clean 1440p". Each
     clip is saved in the recordings folder: one side is the console's own picture (labelled
     "Console"), the other what PushFPS shows. Watch them full screen. That, not a number, is how you
     judge it; nothing here may say "better" before you have.
  4.   Run-First-Test.cmd -Session builds          about 15 minutes: this download with everything off
                                                   against Package 7 (and the original and stable copy).
  Each session ends with RESULT.txt on screen and ONE zip to send back. That is all that is needed.
  Wired or Wi-Fi both run; on Wi-Fi every table says "Wi-Fi: diagnostic only".

WHAT IS IN PACKAGE 8A    (the first of three downloads; all in the experimental build, OFF until chosen)
  - Restoration: a clean-up stage for the compressed 1080p stream in front of the upscaler to
    2560x1440, 1080p to 1440p, HDR handled like the rest of the picture (the colour is checked only
    by tests on GitHub, not on your screen yet), and the picture profiles. Session: visual.
  - The kit: the visual and builds sessions; time estimates taken from your runs of 20 November;
    RESULT.txt has a section MEASURED VALUES for every session.
KNOWN ISSUE IN 8A

    Smooth pacing is still how you want it (quick setup) before you measure anything else.
  - In the tables, the rows "in effect at the end" for the neural layer read n/a: it is not in 8A.
